## 2. Stage format
### Play-In (15-18/10/2026, Riot Games Arena, Los Angeles)
- 4 teams: Karmine Corp (LEC #3), Cloud9 Kia (LCS #3), MVK Esports (LCP #3), FURIA (CBLOL #2). Fully random draw held 10/10 after the CBLOL final (no pots, no region limits).
- Double elimination, every series Bo5, Fearless Draft, 6 series in 4 days. One winner goes to Swiss Tier 4; others finish 17th-19th (prize $50k/$40k/$30k).
- Round 1 (15/10): KC vs C9 and FURIA vs MVK. R2 (16/10): winners' match (R1 winners) and elimination match (R1 losers). R3 (17/10): loser of winners' match vs elimination-match winner (loser 18th). R4 (18/10): winners' match winner vs R3 winner; winner advances, loser 17th. Source for R2-R4 mapping: AccountShark https://accountshark.net/blog/lol-worlds-2026-play-in-preview-c9-kc-furia-mvk-schedule-ct-times (secondary; consistent with esports.gg bracket labels https://esports.gg/guides/league-of-legends/worlds-2026-hub/). A bracket reset in the final is not mentioned (UNKNOWN).

### Swiss Stage (23-31/10/2026, CUTX Event Center, Allen, Texas, ~8,100-8,600)
- 16 teams (15 direct + Play-In winner), up to 5 rounds, pairing by identical record, no rematches. 3 wins = advance, 3 losses = out; 8 advance.
- Bo1 by default; Bo3 whenever a result would qualify or eliminate a team.
- Round 1: random draw, Tier 1 v Tier 4 and Tier 2 v Tier 3, no same-region pairing. Same-region pairings allowed from Round 2.
- Seed origin of tiers (Sheep Esports rulebook summary https://www.sheepesports.com/es/articles/worlds-2026-swiss-stage-seeding-who-will-have-to-go-through-the-play-in/en): Tier 1 = LCK1, LPL1, LCS1, LEC1; Tier 2 = LCP1, CBLOL1, LCK2, LPL2; Tier 3 = LCS2, LEC2, LCK3, LPL3; Tier 4 = LCP2, LPL4, LCK4, Play-In winner.
- Tiers: T1 Gen.G, Anyone's Legend, Team Liquid Alienware, G2. T2 LOS, Team Secret Whales, Hanwha Life, Bilibili Gaming. T3 LYON, Movistar KOI, T1, TOP Esports. T4 CTBC Flying Oyster, Invictus Gaming, Dplus KIA, Play-In winner.
- Same-region Round 1 exclusions I derived (not quoted): GEN not DK; AL not IG; HLE not T1; BLG not TES; Play-In winner KC not G2, C9 not TLAW.
- **Round 1 draw: NOT published.** Scheduled 18/10 (Inven https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26821/worlds-2026-cheat-sheet-schedule-teams-format-and-pickems ; AccountShark). Later draws (Inven): 23/10, 24/10, 26/10, 30/10; which round each is UNKNOWN.

### Knockouts
- 8 teams, single elimination, all Bo5, no bronze match (LolTheory, secondary). QF draw on 31/10: the two 3-0 teams each face a random 3-2 team on opposite sides; the other four are paired at random (primer).
- QF 03-06/11 and SF 07-08/11, CUTX Event Center, Allen. Final 14/11, Barclays Center, Brooklyn.

## 4. Pick'Ems (2026)
Where: https://lolesports.com/pickems or the League client; Riot account + summoner name needed; unavailable in China and other locally published regions plus a few sanctioned territories (esportnow https://esportnow.gg/lol/news/worlds-2026-pickems-crystal-ball-rewards-deadline).
- **Stage points (official lolesports page, read 15/10): Play-Ins 35, Swiss 125, Knockouts 160** (bracket total 320, my sum). Source https://lolesports.com/en-US/pickems/115660540725177488
- **Locks (Riot primer, all "before the first match" of the stage):** Play-In 15/10 11:00 PDT = **16/10 07:00 NZDT**; Swiss 23/10 10:00 PDT = 24/10 06:00 NZDT (opens 18/10; the live page and some outlets say 19/10); Knockouts 03/11 14:00 PST = 04/11 11:00 NZDT (opens 31/10; live page 01/11). Each stage is independent; a missed stage only costs that stage. Scores finalise after the final, rewards within 48 h, contact Player Support if missing by 21/11.
- **Picks per stage and per-pick scoring: not officially verified.** Third-party descriptions only: Play-In is a bracket pick of the 6 double-elim series, 5 points each and 10 for the deciding final (lolpickem https://lolpickem.com/worlds-pickems/ ); Swiss is picking the 8 teams that reach 3 wins in 3-0/3-1/3-2 slots, ~10 per correct advancer plus an exact-record bonus (BoostingMarket https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-worlds-2026-pickems-guide/ , says it read the official page in September); Knockouts weight later rounds higher with the champion pick largest. **0-3 picks and a "no duplicate team in 3-0 and 0-3" restriction: UNKNOWN** (no 2026 source mentions 0-3 picks; do not assume the 2025 rule).
- Rewards (official page): Perfect = Ezreal and Udyr icons plus 12 more rewards (other outlets say seven Ultimate skins; conflicting), Top 5,000 = True Damage Yasuo, Top 25% = Spicy Dog emote, Top 50% = Groovy Pup icon, any pick = Laying It Down emote.
- Date conflicts to ignore: esports.gg https://esports.gg/guides/league-of-legends/worlds-2026-pickems/ says lock "16 Oct 11:20 AM PDT" and Knockouts open 1 Nov to 25 Nov; this disagrees with Riot's primer.
