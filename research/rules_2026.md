**New for 2026 and relevant to game-1 side/pick modelling: "First Selection" / Right of First Selection (RoFS).** WCE ruleset 4.1.4 (accessed 15/10/2026): for every match except Play-In Round 4, one team gets RoFS for Game 1 and chooses EITHER pick position (first/second pick) OR map side; the opponent gets its preference on the other. Play-In R1-R3: coin flip. Play-In R4 (the final): the team arriving from the winners' match (Round 2 Match 1) gets Right of Dual Selection, i.e. both side and pick order. Swiss: RoFS to the higher regional seed, coin flip if equal. QF: RoFS to the better Swiss record, draw order if equal. SF and Final: coin flip. Games 2+: the loser of the previous game has RoFS. Riot's season article confirms this replaced side selection league-wide from the start of 2026 [https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/season-start-2026-lol-esports, 08/01/2026, accessed 15/10/2026].

## What this changes for the model
1. **Draft mode is stage-dependent and the model should treat it as such.** Standard draft for 11 of the Swiss matches (R1 x8, R2 x8 are Bo1, and the four 1-1 Bo1s in R3: 20 Bo1s in total), Fearless for every Bo3 and Bo5 (Play-In, Swiss R3 top/bottom, R4, R5, knockouts). If the ratings carry a "Fearless depth" adjustment (champion-pool breadth, game-3+ win rate), apply it only to Bo3/Bo5 series. Fearless is hard Fearless: both teams lose picked champions, bans reset each game, pool resets each series.
2. **Game-1 side/pick advantage changed.** With RoFS a team chooses side OR pick order, not side alone. Any blue-side win-rate adjustment for game 1 should be halved or dropped; in Swiss the higher regional seed holds RoFS, in the Play-In final the winners' bracket team gets both.
3. **Pick'Ems optimisation:** Play-In 5/5/5/5/5/10 and Knockouts 10/10/10/10/20/20/80 can be used as given (both match official totals). Swiss: 10 per advancer is solid; do not model an 0-3 pick and do not assume a flat 15-point exact-record bonus (45 points of bonus over 8 slots is uneven). Optimise Swiss on advancers first and treat record bonuses as a tie-breaker until the live tooltip is read. Crystal Ball cannot be reopened; the 1,050 split is unverified and should not drive bracket decisions.
4. **Swiss simulation:** Use the exact round structure from the WCE ruleset (R3 Bo3 only for 2-0 and 0-2; six Bo3 in R4; three in R5), enforce no rematches across all five rounds, draw-order ties irrelevant. QF draw constraint: 3-0 teams face random 3-2 teams on opposite halves; the leftover 3-2 team is in the pool of four with the three 3-1 teams.
5. **TES:** keep 369 at top; add a staleness/uncertainty flag; keep a ZUIAN-return branch (if his third interview succeeds he must start immediately). Coaching staff absence is an unverified extra downside.
6. **Patch 26.20 confirmed officially**, so any meta adjustment (Ambessa and K'Sante nerfs per secondary outlets) can be anchored to it. Prize pool and split are now official and can be used for expected-value framing.
