# Patches 26.18 to 26.20: every Summoner's Rift change before Worlds 2026

_Compiled 15/10/2026 (dd/mm/yyyy). Machine-readable copy: data/patch_state.json. Source of every number: the official patch notes on leagueoflegends.com (URLs in Sources). Secondary cross-check only: LoL wiki V26.18, V26.19, V26.20._

## Worlds patch

**Worlds 2026 is played on patch 26.20.** The official 26.20 notes are subtitled "the Worlds patch is here!" and the lead paragraph says: "For Summoner's Rift, this is the patch we'll be seeing our year-defining competitive play on." The 26.19 notes say: "We're soon coming up on the Worlds patch (which is the patch after this one), so our first round of pro-focused Summoner's Rift changes are coming up here."

## How to read the direction column

Riot does not put a buff/nerf label on each champion. **Label = official** means Riot's blurb or intro explicitly calls that change a buff, nerf or adjustment. **Label = inferred** means the direction was decided from the numbers plus the stated intent in the blurb (quoted in the Notes column of the detail tables). "adjust" is used only where power moves sideways (Cassiopeia 26.18, Lucian 26.19, Rocketbelt, support items). Classic, ARAM Mayhem and Arena changes are excluded.

## Direction ambiguities

- Ambessa and K'Sante (26.20): Riot's intro calls them "adjustments to some pro staples" but every number goes down; recorded as nerf (inferred).
- Vayne (26.20): Riot says "adjusting her base stats"; regen is higher until roughly level 16 and 0.2 lower at 18, Q mana is a straight reduction; recorded as buff (inferred).
- Hextech Rocketbelt (26.20): less haste, more active damage; Riot frames it as pulling back generic value in favour of all-ins; recorded as adjust (the existing dashboard table calls it rework).
- Rumble (26.19): overheat attack speed down for everyone, monster cap up for jungle; Riot's intent is to hit elite-level lane Rumble; recorded as nerf.
- Ryze (26.19): Q and E nerfed, armor growth up; Riot's intent is "restraints on the archmage"; recorded as nerf.
- Vi (26.19): base AD and shield down, AD growth up; Riot's intent is a "riskier pick"; recorded as nerf.
- Lucian (26.19): passive down, Q up, Riot says "adjust"; recorded as adjust (official). Riot's 26.20 blurb later says it landed as a bot-lane nerf, then buffed in 26.20.
- Cassiopeia (26.18): early nerf, late buff, Riot says "adjustments"; recorded as adjust (official). Then nerfed outright in 26.20, so her Worlds-patch state is nerf.
- Zaahen (26.18): Riot says it "might read as a nerf" but the Q2 damage rises and Q returns after resurrection; recorded as buff (inferred).
- Master Yi (26.19): Riot says "minimal impact on Summoner's Rift"; still a buff by the numbers.
