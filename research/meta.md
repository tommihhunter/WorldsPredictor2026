## 1. Patch and rules

**Patch: 26.20** (the "Worlds patch"). Released 07/10/2026 (NZ morning of 07/10; Riot notes timestamp 06/10/2026 18:00 UTC).
- Official notes: https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-20-notes/ (fetched and read in full 15/10/2026). Riot's intro: "For Summoner's Rift, this is the patch we'll be seeing our year-defining competitive play on."
- Corroboration that Worlds is played on 26.20: Dot Esports https://dotesports.com/league-of-legends/guides/lol-patch-26-20-notes ; Sheep Esports https://www.sheepesports.com/en/articles/lol-patch-26-20-preview-more-lillia-buffs-rocketbelt-receives-another-adjustment/en ; Strafe https://www.strafe.com/news/read/league-of-legends-worlds-2026-play-in-pick-ems-guide/
- **Does the patch change for Swiss/Knockouts? UNKNOWN.** No source says Worlds moves to 26.21 (a third-party patch calendar lists 26.21 = 21/10/2026 and 26.22 = 04/11/2026: https://wecoach.gg/blog/article/lol-2026-patch-schedule , low weight). All sources that address it say "Worlds is played on 26.20" with no stated switch. Treat as single-patch event unless Riot announces otherwise.

### Main changes in 26.20 (official notes)
- **Nerfs:** Ambessa (passive 5-30 +25% bAD to 5-25 +20%), Ashe (AD/level 3.5 to 3), Cassiopeia (base HP 630 to 610, mana 480 to 450; "another swing" after earlier nerfs because she is "still dominant in early laning"), K'Sante (W mana +5 each rank, W DR 30/75% to 25/70%), Yunara (Q on-hit -2/rank; W no longer stacks with other slows), Runaan's Hurricane (bolt AD 65% to 60%), Experimental Hexplate (ranged mod 70% to 50%).
- **Buffs:** Diana (W shield ratio 11% to 14%), Kennen (E AS +10%), Kindred (W monster mod 150% to 165%, E slow 1s to 1.5s), Lillia (R sleep 2.5s to 2.5/2.75/3s), Lucian (AD/level 2.5 to 2.9), Mordekaiser (Q late scaling, R cooldown), Neeko (E damage/root), Smolder (crit scaling, R heal), Swain (MR/armor, W), Tahm Kench (Q heal), Vayne (HP5, flat 30 Q mana).
- **Item system:** Hextech Rocketbelt ability haste 20 to 10, active 100 (+10% AP) to 135 (+15% AP), pushing it from "escape" toward AP-bruiser all-in tool.
- **Pulled from patch:** planned Varus and Sylas changes (Dot Esports, Sheep).
- Riot's stated intent (Phroxzon quote via EarlyGame https://earlygame.com/news/lol/lol-patch-26-20-this-is-the-worlds-patch): loosen coordinated drafts without resetting the meta; remove reliable pro blind picks (K'Sante, Ambessa) and "handshake hyperscaler" Yunara.
- Non-balance: replays now only available for matches you played; third-party replay download removed (Insider Gaming https://insider-gaming.com/lol-patch-26-20-every-change-impact-worlds-meta/ ; not verified in the official text I read, treat as secondary).

### Worlds-specific rules
- **Fearless Draft: YES in Bo3/Bo5 (all Play-In and Knockout series, and Swiss Bo3s).** A champion picked in an earlier game of a series cannot be picked again by either team in that series. Not applicable to Swiss Bo1. Evidence: Insider Gaming Play-In page calls it "Fearless Bo5" https://insider-gaming.com/league-of-legends-worlds-2026-teams-play-in-draw-pools/ ; LolTheory https://blog.loltheory.gg/how-lol-worlds-works/ ; AccountShark https://accountshark.net/blog/lol-worlds-2026-schedule-format-teams-guide ; Sheep (Fearless "will remain in effect for 2026", 07/11/2025) https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/lol-worlds-headed-to-texas-first-stand-expands-to-eight-teams-riot-outlines-the-2026/en . gol.gg tags every 2026 domestic playoff and MSI 2026 as "Fearless Draft". The official Worlds rulebook was NOT retrieved; confidence: high but not first-party.
- **First Selection (new 2026 rule, all regions):** the team earning selection picks either side or draft priority, the opponent gets the other (GosuGamers https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/77812-lol-esports-2026-introduces-new-draft-rule-first-selection-and-a-bo5-heavy-first-stand). At Worlds Play-In, AccountShark reports the winners-bracket side in Round 4 gets "Right of Dual Selection" for Game 1 (secondary, unverified).
- **New objectives / disabled champions at Worlds 2026: UNKNOWN / none found.** No source mentions a Worlds-specific disabled champion list or a new objective. Official notes mention "Mayhem" and "Classic" mode content only (not relevant to Worlds).

## 3. Meta identity

**Data read (inference from the gol.gg numbers above, not an analyst quote):** the pre-Worlds environment is ban-heavy on early-power champions (Cassiopeia, Nocturne, Vi banned in roughly 3 of 4, 2 of 3, and 1 of 2 games respectively), junglers with early skirmish/gank kits dominate presence (Vi, Jarvan IV, Lee Sin, Pantheon, Naafiri, Qiyana, Nocturne), control/utility mages hold mid (Orianna, Ryze, Syndra, Viktor, plus Akali/Locke as assassin counters), and bot lane leans enchanter/engage supports (Seraphine, Lulu, Karma, Nautilus, Alistar). Mean game length ~32.5 minutes, so games are neither very short nor stall-heavy.

**What analysts say is rewarded in 26.20:**
- Hotspawn (https://www.hotspawn.com/league-of-legends/news/worlds-2026-meta-analysis): aggressive early bot lane (Yunara nerfed so bot is not "a waiting game"; Lucian buffed); bot "quite skirmish-heavy" and conceding two early drakes "could be fatal"; side-lane wave timing before objective spawns; top lane giving "strong individual players more agency"; mid needs strong fundamentals. NOTE: Hotspawn also lists "Rumble nerfed", "Fiora buffed" and "Draven buffed", none of which appear in the official 26.20 notes (the Fiora change in the notes is in the Classic section and is a nerf). Treat that article's champion lists as unreliable; the style comments are opinion.
- EarlyGame / Riot quote: changes "favor coordinated teams, where small timing gains and draft flexibility matter more" and rising picks are edge-of-viability champions (Lillia, Kennen, Kindred, possibly Diana jungle). https://earlygame.com/news/lol/lol-patch-26-20-this-is-the-worlds-patch
- Insider Gaming: Riot's goal is to curb reliable pro picks and late-game scaling; Rocketbelt change shifts AP bruisers toward aggressive all-ins; Hexplate nerf hits ranged top laners. https://insider-gaming.com/lol-patch-26-20-every-change-impact-worlds-meta/
- Hotspawn Pick'Ems piece: Vi expected to stay popular; Jarvan IV ban rate may fall; Qiyana tipped for highest win rate; "junglers look strong across top teams"; HLE had the fastest MSI game (22:16) and an aggressive jungler. https://www.hotspawn.com/league-of-legends/news/lol-worlds-2026-pickems
- Fearless Draft rewards deep pools and flexibility in Bo3/Bo5 (Sheep: surprises likely "if Fearless Draft produces" them).
