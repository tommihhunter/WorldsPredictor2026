# CFO - CTBC Flying Oyster (LCP, seed 2)
_Last updated 15/10/2026. Sources accessed 15/10/2026 unless noted._

## Headline
Taiwanese LCP runner-up (Split 3) who rebuilt mid and support in May, climbed from 5th-6th finishes in Splits 1 and 2 to the Grand Final (swept 0-3 by TSW), and enter the Swiss Stage directly for a second straight Worlds (QF at Worlds 2025). No 2026 international matches before Worlds.
