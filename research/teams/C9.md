## Identity
- Wins: Vulcan-centred playmaking (KDA 7.3, 81.7% KP, Spring MVP) with Blaber (74.4% KP) enabling Tactical (+319 GD@15) and a strong dragon/herald control profile (56%/60%) despite bad first tower (40%). 7-0 in the Spring regular season; swept Shopify and beat LYON 3-1 in Summer. 0-2 and 0-3 against FURIA in the Americas Cup. [7][8][11]
- Loses: Early lane and jungle deficits (Blaber -496 XPD@15, -6.4 CSD@15; Thanatos -191 GD@15), low damage from the jungle slot (14.5%) and a top-heavy reliance on late objectives; went 3-8 in games in Spring playoffs, 0-3 and 0-3 in back-to-back series. Lost to TL 1-3 and LYON 1-3 in Summer after leading early in the bracket. [8][16]

## Draft tendencies
- Top: Thanatos K'Sante, Camille, Olaf. Jungle: Blaber Nocturne 2-0 (Nocturne is the 2nd most banned champion in LCS Summer PO with 29 bans), Naafiri 2-0, Xin Zhao. Mid: Loki Syndra/Orianna. Bot: Tactical Ezreal 3-0, Yunara 1-2. Support: Vulcan Karma 2-0, Seraphine, Lulu, Rakan. [8][19]
- League-wide most banned in LCS Summer PO: Cassiopeia 37, Nocturne 29, Vi 22, Olaf 21, Caitlyn 17. Team-specific first-pick/ban lines: UNKNOWN. [19]
- C9's only game win in the UB final vs TL (G3) came off a top-lane draft, per AccountShark. [21]

## International pedigree
- Worlds experience: Thanatos and Loki debut; Blaber, Tactical and Vulcan have prior appearances (exact counts UNKNOWN). Cloud9 returns to Worlds for the first time since 2023 (rft.gg). [3][20]
- 2026 vs other regions: Americas Cup only. Series 2-2 (L 0-2 FURIA, W 3-2 RED Canids, W 3-1 Sentinels, L 0-3 FURIA); both losses to the CBLOL champion-to-be. No LCK/LPL/LEC/LCP games in 2026 yet. [10][11]
