## Identity
Wins: early lead into objective conversion. In LEC playoffs G2 averaged GD@15 +1490, 70% first tower, 81% baron and 62% dragon control, with Caps (GD@15 +501, 15 solo kills) and the bot lane (Hans Sama +348) driving leads. Summer playoffs: 9-1, GD@15 +856, first tower 90%, baron 87%. Loses: when behind early against top international teams. Internationally G2 averaged GD@15 -615, first tower 37%, herald 42%, baron 48%; WR when ahead @15 is still ~78% so deficits are the problem. Red side is a stark weakness internationally (4-14, 22%) vs blue 13-4 (76%); in LEC playoffs the gap is small (blue 22-6, red 6-2). Top, bot and support all had negative GD@15 and XPD@15 internationally (BrokenBlade -285, Hans Sama -180, Labrov -75).
