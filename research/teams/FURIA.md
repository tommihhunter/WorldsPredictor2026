# FURIA - FURIA Esports (CBLOL / Brazil, seed 2, Play-In)
_Last updated 15/10/2026. Sources accessed 15/10/2026 unless noted._

## Headline
Americas Cup and CBLOL Split 1 champions, CBLOL Split 2 runner-up (2-3 to LOS on 10/10) and Brazil's seed 2 in the Play-In (opens vs MVK Esports on 15/10, 16:00 PDT); strong domestic record but went 0-2 in series (0-6 in games) at MSI 2026 and 0-2 at EWC.

## 2026 results
- Domestic:
  - CBLOL Cup (Jan-Mar): 3rd (regular season 5-2, 3rd seed); did not qualify for First Stand; lost to RED Canids in the lower bracket (Wikipedia says 1-3, 28/02; ExitLag says 0-2 Bo3 semi; sources conflict). [7][9]
  - CBLOL Split 1 (28/03-06/06): Champion, 3-1 LOS in the final (06/06). Regular season 2nd (5-2). Playoffs (rdy.gg, labelled by inference): 3-0 LOUD (17/05, UB R2), 3-0 RED Canids (24/05, UB final), 3-1 LOS (06/06, grand final). Finals MVP and season MVP Tatu. [8][10]
  - CBLOL Split 2 (25/07-10/10): Runner-up, 2-3 LOS (10/10). Regular season 5-2 (3rd or 4th; sources differ). Playoffs: 3-0 RED (06/09), 1-3 LOS (12/09, UB semi), 3-2 RED (26/09, LB semi, score derived from gol.gg totals), 3-0 LOUD (03/10, LB final), 2-3 LOS (10/10). Seed 2, Play-In. [5][6][11][12]
- International:
  - Americas Cup (04-08/03, São Paulo): Champion, 8-0 in games: 2-0 Cloud9 (04/03), 3-0 Sentinels (UB final), 3-0 Cloud9 (08/03). Prize: Riot-funded bootcamp in South Korea during MSI. [13][14]
  - First Stand 2026: did not qualify (LOUD went). [7]
  - MSI 2026 (Daejeon): 7th-8th, US$110,000. 0-3 LYON (04/07, UB R1), 0-3 T1 (LB R1). [15][16]
  - EWC 2026 (Paris): 13th-16th. 0-1 G2 (Bo1, Group A opener), 0-2 Dplus KIA (elimination). [17][18]

## Identity
- Wins: Early skirmish aggression (first blood 60%, kills/deaths 14.4/9.9, 70% herald control), Tatu (Split 1 Finals MVP) and JoJo as high-KP enablers, and a bot lane built around Ayu (28% DMG). They handled Cloud9 six times across three formats (Sheep) and swept the Americas Cup in March. [5][13]
- Loses: Lane-phase losses (CSD@15 -10.1; -41 at MSI), poor baron control against elite opponents (20% at MSI), and results collapse against LCK/LPL-level opposition: 0-3 vs LYON and 0-3 vs T1 at MSI, 0-1 vs G2 and 0-2 vs Dplus KIA at EWC. Split 2: LOS beat them 3-1 and 3-2. [5][15][17]

## Draft tendencies
- Top: Guigo Ambessa, Olaf, Gwen, Jax, K'Sante, Camille, Ornn, Sion (wide). Jungle: Tatu Xin Zhao 2-0, Trundle, Sejuani, Naafiri, Nasus (Split 1 final: Pantheon, Olaf, Nasus). Mid: Tutsz Ryze, Twisted Fate, Viktor, Annie. Bot: Ayu Ezreal/Kalista (3 games each), Yunara (13/2/5 in the LOUD sweep), Ashe. Support: Karma, Seraphine, Renata Glasc. [5][8][10]
- League-wide CBLOL Split 2 PO bans: Cassiopeia 30, Nocturne 24, Jarvan IV 21. FURIA-specific first-pick/ban rates: UNKNOWN. [19]

## International pedigree
- Worlds experience: Tatu, Tutsz and Ayu are debutants; Guigo and JoJo have prior appearances (counts UNKNOWN). Early Game notes FURIA "gained plenty of international experience during the year." [3][20]
- 2026 vs other regions: Americas Cup 3-0 in series (CBLOL/LCS sides); MSI 0-2 in series (0-6 games); EWC 0-2 in series (0-1 G2, 0-2 Dplus). FURIA's Tatu publicly questioned why LCS gets three Worlds slots to CBLOL's one before the CBLOL got its second slot (22/03). [13][14][15][17]
