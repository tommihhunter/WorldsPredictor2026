## Identity
How they win: Peyz is the hyper-carry (26.2% of gold, 30.3% of damage, +401 GD@15) with Keria (83.3% KP) enabling; Doran has 9 solo kills (most of the four LCK top laners in the samples) and Oner/Peyz create early pressure. They out-scrambled Dplus KIA in long fights (30-13 kills in a 39-minute G3; Peyz pentakill G1) and have won the herald fight 66.7% of the time. [9][12]
How they lose: they fall behind early (GD@15 -468, FT 44.4%, 22.2% FB) and lose Baron and dragon control (37.3% / 42.2%); drafts have been questioned. They also lost their 2-0 lead to HLE (02/09) and were taken out 1-3 by G2 at MSI and 0-1 vs BLG. [9][5][15][47]
