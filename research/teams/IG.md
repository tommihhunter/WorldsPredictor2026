# IG - Invictus Gaming (LPL, seed 4)
_Last updated 15/10/2026. Sources accessed 15/10/2026 unless noted._

## Headline
Fourth LPL seed after a lower-bracket run (Split 3 LBF 2-3 to AL, then 3-1 over JDG in the Regional Finals LBF); veteran roster (TheShy, Rookie, Meiko) that was 5th-6th and 9th-12th in Splits 1 and 2 before a 3rd place in Split 3, with no international play in 2026 [3][9][21].

## Identity
Wins through Wei's jungle tempo (Qiyana, Jarvan IV, Wukong) and veteran mid/support coordination; closes games via Baron and Dragon Soul pushes, ~7k gold leads by 25 min [21]. Loses when out-tempo'd early (1-3 vs TES in UB final; 2-3 vs AL) and had a poor Splits 1-2 (34.9%, 39.5% WR), meaning recent form is a one-split sample [16].

## Draft tendencies
Pools above (Rookie: Ryze/Aurora/Syndra/Viktor/Orianna; Wei: Qiyana/Jarvan/Wukong/Trundle; JiaQi: Tristana/Ezreal/Yunara). First-pick/bans: UNKNOWN.
