# TLAW - Team Liquid Alienware (LCS / North America, seed 1)
_Last updated 15/10/2026. Sources accessed 15/10/2026 unless noted._

## Headline
Reigning LCS Summer champions (3-1 over LYON on 04/10/2026), NA's number one seed, straight into the Swiss Stage (Pool 1) after a strong playoff run, but only 6-6 at MSI 2026 (out in the Play-In, 9th) and missed Worlds 2025.

## Identity
- Wins: Josedeodo-centred jungle (Qiyana, Lee Sin, Vi) with the best early GD/XP in the three NA teams, Morgan as a reliable carry-top (KDA 6.6), and CoreJJ's high-KP engage support. Spawn describes two modes: a methodical, zone-based game and a faster, fight-everything game driven by Josedeodo and Quid, picked by game pace. First tower 72.7% and baron 77.3% show strong objective conversion once ahead (100% when ahead at 15). [23]
- Loses: when behind early and when Quid falls behind in lane (Quid -309 GD@15, -6.3 CSD@15). At MSI they were out-fought early (FB 25%, GD@15 -184) and went 0-6 in games against T1. Their only domestic playoff games lost came to Cloud9's top-lane draft (G3 of the UB final) and LYON's jungle-led G3. [4][7][15]

## Draft tendencies
- League-wide (LCS Summer PO, gol.gg): Cassiopeia (37 bans) and Nocturne (29) are the most banned; this is the context TL drafts into. TL-specific ban/first-pick data: UNKNOWN. [25]
- Comfort/flex: Josedeodo Qiyana 3-0, Lee Sin 2-0 (KDA 25.0 on a 2-game sample), Vi 1-1; Morgan Camille 3-0 and Ambessa 2-0; Yeon Ashe/Lucian 2-0 each; CoreJJ Nautilus 2-0; Quid Orianna/Galio 2-0. Wide pools (7-9 champions per player in 11 games) with no obvious single comfort pick to ban out. [9]-[13]
- Draft lead: Spookz runs draft prep with daily discussions; Algbory handles scouting with SAP. [23]

## International pedigree
