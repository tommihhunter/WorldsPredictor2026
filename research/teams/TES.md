# TES - Top Esports (LPL, seed 3)
_Last updated 15/10/2026. Sources accessed 15/10/2026 unless noted._

## Headline
Split 2 runners-up and 2025 Worlds semifinalists who scraped into Worlds via the Regional Finals (3-1 over IG) after a tie for 7th in Split 3; they go in with 369 replacing visa-blocked top laner ZUIAN and a coaching-visa question mark [4][13][19].

  - LPL Split 1: 5th-6th. Beat WE 3-2 (Play-In 09/02), lost 2-3 to JDG (24/02), beat WE 3-0 (28/02), lost 2-3 to WBG (02/03) [6].
  - LPL Split 2: 2nd. Beat LGD 3-0 (29/05), JDG 3-1 (31/05), WE 3-0 (UBF 07/06), lost Grand Final 0-3 to BLG (14/06) [7].
  - LPL Split 3: 7th-8th (tied for 7th). Group Ascend 9-5 (2nd/3rd). Lost UB R1 2-3 to LGD (29/08), lost LB R1 2-3 to IG (05/09) [5][10].
  - LPL Regional Finals: beat IG 3-1 (17/09) for seed 3 [4].
- International:
  - First Stand 2026: did not qualify (LPL reps BLG, JDG) [12].
  - MSI 2026: lost BR1 2-3 to G2 (03/07), lost 1-3 to Team Secret Whales (05/07); eliminated [13].
  - EWC 2026: did not play (AL, BLG, JDG were the LPL teams) [14].

## Identity
Skill-heavy mid/bot (Creme, JackeyLove top DPM at 714/839) with Tian's high kill participation; won Split 2 UB path via aggressive jungle plays (Tian Pantheon 8/1/11 vs IG, Creme Locke 13/2/8) [4]. Loses to disciplined macro teams (BLG 0-3 final, 0-2 regular-season), loses close series (2-3 vs LGD, 2-3 vs IG, 2-3 vs G2) and struggled at MSI (3-6 games) [13].

## Draft tendencies
Pools above (Creme: Ahri/Orianna/Syndra/Hwei/Aurora; JackeyLove: Ezreal/Jhin/Lucian; Tian: Lee Sin/Jarvan IV/Pantheon/Wukong). Top lane will be a new look with 369 (Gnar/Rumble/Gangplank historically). First-pick/bans: UNKNOWN.
