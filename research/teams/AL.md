# AL - Anyone's Legend (LPL, seed 1)
_Last updated 15/10/2026. Sources accessed 15/10/2026 unless noted._

## Headline
LPL Split 3 champions (3-1 over BLG on 13/09/2026) and LPL seed 1, but with no First Stand or MSI appearance in 2026 and a 0-2 EWC quarterfinal exit; they had lost six straight series to BLG before the final [3][7][14].

## 2026 results
- Domestic:
  - LPL Split 1: 4th. Lost LB R3 1-3 to WBG (05/03); earlier lost UB R2 2-3 to JDG (26/02), beat WE 3-0 (24/02), beat IG 3-1 (03/03) [6]. Group Ascend 1st (8-2) [5].
  - LPL Split 2: 4th. Lost UB R2 0-3 to WE (01/06), beat EDG 3-0, LGD 3-0, then lost LB R3 0-3 to BLG (08/06) [7].
  - LPL Split 3: 1st (champions). Group Ascend 9-5 (2nd/3rd on tiebreaks, sources differ), bye to UB R2: beat LGD 3-1 (04/09), lost UBF 0-3 to BLG (07/09), beat IG 3-2 in LBF (12/09), beat BLG 3-1 in Grand Final (13/09). Tarzan Finals MVP [9][19][20].
- International:
  - First Stand 2026: did not qualify (LPL reps were BLG and JDG) [12].
  - MSI 2026: did not qualify (LPL reps BLG, TES) [13].
  - EWC 2026 (Paris, 15-19/07): Group A: beat Dplus KIA 1-0 and G2 1-0 (Bo1s), then lost QF 0-2 to Karmine Corp (18/07). Finished 5th-8th [14][15].

## Identity
Wins through jungle-led early tempo and engage synergy between Tarzan and Kael (described as "near-perfect synergy"), snowballing fast: Grand Final wins came in ~30-31 min with kill leads such as 18-8, 20-1, 22-5 [3][19]. Hope is the damage carry (13/0/5 pentakill game). Loses when BLG's macro/lane control denies the early snowball (0-3 UBF; six straight series losses to BLG before 13/09) and when a more disciplined team like KC takes control around 15 min (0-2 at EWC) [14][15][20].
