# BLG - Bilibili Gaming (LPL, seed 2)
_Last updated 15/10/2026. Sources accessed 15/10/2026 unless noted._

## Headline
Best-credentialed LPL team of 2026 (First Stand champions, Split 1 and 2 champions, MSI runners-up 2-3 to HLE) but lost the Split 3 final 1-3 to AL after a 3-0 upper-final win, exited EWC in the quarterfinal, and has an unsettled top-lane situation [3][7][8][12].

## 2026 results

  - LPL Split 1: 1st. Beat NIP 3-0, WBG 3-0, JDG 3-2 (UBF 04/03), then Grand Final 3-1 vs JDG (08/03). Group Ascend 6-4 [5][6][25].
  - LPL Split 2: 1st. Lost UB R1 1-3 to WE (30/05), then LB run: EDG 3-0, JDG 3-1, AL 3-0, WE 3-2 (LBF 13/06), Grand Final 3-0 vs TES (14/06, Wuhan). Group Ascend 12-2 (1st) [7][11].
  - LPL Split 3: 2nd. Group Ascend 12-2 (1st). Bye, beat WE 3-1 (03/09), beat AL 3-0 (UBF 07/09), lost Grand Final 1-3 to AL (13/09) [5][8].
- International:
  - First Stand 2026 (Sao Paulo): CHAMPIONS. Group A: 3-2 BNK, 3-0 G2; SF 3-0 JDG (21/03); Final 3-1 G2 (22/03) [12][26].
  - MSI 2026: runners-up. BR1 3-2 T1 (04/07), BR2 3-0 LYON (06/07), UB final 3-1 HLE (09/07), Final 2-3 HLE (12/07) after leading 2-1 [13].
  - EWC 2026: Group C Bo1s: beat Movistar KOI 1-0, beat T1 1-0; QF lost 1-2 to Dplus KIA (18/07); 5th-8th [14][15].

## Identity
Wins through top-lane lane dominance (Bin +340 to +490 gold and +12 to +14 CS at 15 across both international events), Knight's control and Xun's tempo; closed games with global pressure comps (Pantheon, Galio at First Stand) [26][17]. Loses to coordinated or disciplined opponents in long series: MSI Final 2-3 after 2-1 lead; EWC QF 1-2; Split 3 Final 1-3 [13][15]. Viper's early numbers at 15 are negative at MSI (-514), suggesting a scaling bot lane.

Champion pools above. Bin: Renekton/Gnar/K'Sante/Yorick/Sion; Knight: Ryze/Aurora/Viktor/Syndra/Cassiopeia; Viper: Ezreal/Ashe/Mel/Yunara. First-pick priorities and flex/ban data: UNKNOWN.

## International pedigree
- 2026: First Stand title (12-3 in games), MSI final (11-6), EWC QF (3-2); record 26-11 vs LCK/LEC/LCS/LCP/CBLOL opposition. Series vs LCK: beat BNK 3-2, T1 3-2 (MSI R1), HLE 3-1 then lost 2-3 final; lost EWC QF to DK 1-2 [13][14].
- Worlds: 3rd-4th 2023, 2nd 2024, Swiss exit 2025; fourth consecutive Worlds in 2026 [10][23]. Viper won Worlds 2021 (EDG) and is the first back-to-back First Stand winner with different teams [12][14].

- Reported coordination problems from Xun after EWC [12].
