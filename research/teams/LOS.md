_Last updated 15/10/2026. Sources accessed 15/10/2026 unless noted._

## Headline
CBLOL Split 2 champions (3-2 over FURIA, 10/10/2026, Rio de Janeiro) and Brazil's seed 1 straight into the Swiss Stage (Pool 2), a CBLOL "guest team" with an all-new five of whom four are Worlds debutants; strong early-game gold (+846 GD@15) but still unproven internationally (EWC 9th-12th as "MIBR.LOS").

## Identity
- Wins: Zest's lane dominance (+13.4 CSD@15, +497 GD@15, 5 solo kills), Feisty as a hyper-efficient mid (KDA 5.2, 75% KP, Orianna/Ryze/Galio unbeaten) and Ackerman's playmaking from support; they convert leads (88.9% win rate when ahead at 15, first tower 71.4%) and take Nashor (65.5%) in long games. [4]
- Loses: Almost no herald control (23.1%, 0.21 per game), low first blood (35.7%), jungler Curse is behind on CS/XP at 15 (-5.5/-101) despite 68.6% KP, and they needed 5 games against both LOUD and FURIA in the Split 2 playoffs; FURIA beat them 3-1 in the Split 1 final and pushed the Split 2 final to 5. [4][6][7][13]

## Draft tendencies
- Top: Zest Rumble, Jayce, Varus, Ambessa, Gnar (very wide). Mid: Feisty Orianna 3-0, Ryze 2-0, Galio 2-0 (Feisty's pool is the priority to deny).
