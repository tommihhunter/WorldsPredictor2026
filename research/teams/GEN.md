## Identity
How they win: control the map through the first half (61.7% dragon, 74.2% Nashor, +506 GD@15 and 85.7% WR when ahead @15), then convert with a carry ADC who takes 31.7% of damage and 25.4% of gold (Ruler) behind a high-participation support (Duro 79.7% KP) and a jungler involved in 72.3% of kills. [7] Grand Final games 2-4 were 30, 26 and 28 minutes; they closed fast once ahead (e.g. a Zeri + Yuumi pairing in G3, Chovy Lissandra in G2). [3] They also win long fights (45-minute G2 of the UB final vs HLE). [11]
How they lose: International opponents have punished them (0-3 vs G2 at First Stand; 1-2 vs Dplus KIA at EWC, with a 52-minute G1). [14][22] Their only playoff losses were two games to HLE: a 38:45 G1 in the final where HLE won a Baron fight (towers 3-9, gold 72.6k vs 76.3k) and G1 of the UB final (late Baron fight). [3][11][6] First blood rate is low (18.2%) and herald control is below 50%, so early skirmish pressure is not their route. [7]
