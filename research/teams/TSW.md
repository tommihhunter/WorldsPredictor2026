## Identity
- Wins: early-game aggression with Hizto as playmaker, strong first tower (88.1% win rate with it) and baron conversion (98.4%) domestically; heavy objective trade volume (8.18 towers/map, 1.05 barons/map last 3 months). Won MSI game 1 vs TES 29-6 kills ([11][19][22][31]).
- Loses: against disciplined international teams. 0-3 vs G2, BNK FearX, HLE, LYON; a "wave management" weakness is cited for EWC; Hizto described as young and mistake-prone ([14][31][35]). Also dropped to CFO in Split 3 seeding (2-3) so can be pushed by a team that skirmishes well.
