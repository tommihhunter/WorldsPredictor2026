## Identity
How they win: win lanes and CS (CSD@15 +26.3, +1198 GD@15), a mid/ADC duo that carries (ShowMaker 75.7% KP, 27.5% DMG; Smash 28.5% DMG, +443 GD@15), and clutch teamfights/objective steals (Lucid steal vs T1, ShowMaker in the Dragon Pit fight, Smash's Lucian/Jhin/Miss Fortune performances in the qualifier). The coach blends T1's tempo, Gen.G's formations and HLE's bold calls, and the squad has been praised for composure and "recovering from mistakes". [30][12][29]
How they lose: convert poorly (46.2% WR when ahead @15, 47.9% baron control) and Siwoo's low KP (38.2%, KDA 1.9, Jayce 0-3) leaves the top lane isolated; Siwoo + Career produced 12 deaths in the lopsided G1 of the qualifier. They lost 0-3 to KT and 1-3 to T1 in the same bracket and are "inconsistent against top-three opponents". [10][29][41]
