# MVK - MVK Esports (formerly Vikings Esports) (LCP, seed 3, Play-In)
_Last updated 15/10/2026. Sources accessed 15/10/2026 unless noted._

## Headline
Young all-Vietnamese LCP third seed (qualified on 76 Championship Points after losing 0-3 and 1-3 to CFO in Split 3 playoffs) starting in the 4-team Bo5 Play-In (only one team reaches the Swiss Stage) vs FURIA first, with Cloud9 and Karmine Corp in the same bracket; no 2026 international matches yet.

- Media characterisation (subjective): "volatile map movement", lacks macro discipline against structured early-game teams; Hotspawn calls them very young and unproven internationally ([35][36]). Tendency to need scrappy Bo5s: 3-2 wins over CFO (Split 2), DCG and GAM.
- Wins: first baron conversion (82.1% WR when first baron), recovered from 7th in Split 1; beat GAM 3-2 and CFO 3-2 in key Bo5s ([2][20][24]).
- Loses: lowest-rated top (KDA 1.88); lost 0-3 and 1-3 to CFO and 0-3 to TSW/GAM in key playoff series; 2-5 in Split 1 ([1][3][20]).
