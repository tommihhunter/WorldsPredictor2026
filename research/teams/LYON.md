# LYON - LYON (Lyon Gaming) (LCS / North America, seed 2)
_Last updated 15/10/2026. Sources accessed 15/10/2026 unless noted._

## Headline
Winner of LCS Lock-In and Spring, runner-up in Summer (1-3 to Team Liquid Alienware), First Stand group-stage exit, MSI 2026 third place (the best LCS international finish since 2019 per GosuGamers), then a 0-2 group exit at EWC; directly in the Swiss Stage (Pool 3) as NA seed 2.

## 2026 results
- Domestic:
  - LCS Lock-In: Champion, 3-1 Cloud9 KIA in the final (01/03). Path: Swiss 1-2 (5th), LB: 3-0 FlyQuest (21/02), 3-1 Team Liquid (27/02), 3-1 Sentinels (28/02), GF 3-1 C9. Season MVP Inspired. [6][16]
  - LCS Spring: Champion, 3-0 Team Liquid Alienware (14/06). Regular season 5-2 (2nd). Playoffs: 3-2 TL (24/05), 3-0 C9 (07/06, UB final), GF 3-0 TL. MVP Saint (Sheep). [7][8][12]
  - LCS Summer: Runner-up, 1-3 TL (04/10). Regular season 6-1 (1st, 13-2 in games). Playoffs: lost 1-3 to C9 (19/09, UB semi; LYON chose C9), 3-1 Shopify (26/09), 3-1 C9 (03/10, LB final), GF 1-3 TL. NA seed 2. [4][5][13]
- International:
  - First Stand 2026 (São Paulo, 16-22/03): 5th-6th (group B). 3-2 LOUD (17/03), 0-3 Gen.G (19/03), 1-3 JD Gaming (20/03). [14]
  - MSI 2026 (Daejeon): 3rd place, US$240,000. Bracket: 3-0 FURIA (04/07), 0-3 BLG (UB R2), 3-0 Team Secret Whales (08/07), 3-0 G2 (10/07), 2-3 Hanwha Life (11/07, LB final). [17]-[21]
  - EWC 2026 (Paris, 15-19/07): 13th-16th. 0-1 JD Gaming (Bo1), 0-2 MIBR.LOS (15/07, elimination). [22][23]

## Identity
- Wins: Saint (28.6% DMG, +507 GD@15) and Berserker (24.3% gold share) as the damage core, early-game jungle tempo from Inspired (+628 XPD@15 in sample), and first blood/first tower pressure (62.5% each in the playoffs; 76.5% first tower at MSI). Their MSI run showed they can roll sides (3-0 over FURIA, TSW, G2). Won 5 straight NA titles (Inspired) until 04/10. [4][17]
- Loses: Top lane is a liability (Dhokla KDA 1.9, -206 GD@15, 48% KP); objective control drops in series they lose (dragon 41%, herald 44%, baron 41% in playoffs). Lost 0-3 to BLG and 2-3 to Hanwha Life at MSI, 0-3 to Gen.G and 1-3 to JD Gaming at First Stand: they have not beaten an LCK or LPL team in a series in 2026 (they did take games). EWC saw a 0-1 and 0-2 exit. [14][17][22]

## Draft tendencies
- Mid priority: Saint played Syndra, Twisted Fate, Ryze, Locke, Viktor, Annie, Akali, Galio in 8 games (very wide, mostly one-offs). Bot: Berserker Kalista/Yunara/Jayce/Sivir. Jungle: Inspired Wukong/Skarner/Xin Zhao/Lee Sin/Pantheon. Support: Renata Glasc 2-0, Lulu, Alistar 0-2. [9]
- League-wide ban context: Cassiopeia (37 bans) and Nocturne (29) top the ban list in LCS Summer PO. Team-specific ban data: UNKNOWN. [24]
- Game 2 of the grand final featured a Twisted Fate ultimate misfire by Saint that the team attributed to a "Clamp cast target setting" (player error, not a bug). [3]

## International pedigree
- Worlds experience: Saint is a debutant; Dhokla, Inspired, Berserker, Isles are not debutants but exact counts are UNKNOWN. Berserker won two NA titles with Cloud9 and played LCK with DN Freecs last year (Sheep pre-season). [2][10]
- 2026 vs other regions: First Stand 1-2 (W: LOUD 3-2; L: Gen.G 0-3, JDG 1-3). MSI 4-2 in series (W: FURIA 3-0, TSW 3-0, G2 3-0; L: BLG 0-3, HLE 2-3), best LCS MSI placing since 2019. EWC 0-2 (JDG Bo1, MIBR.LOS 0-2). Series versus LCK/LPL teams: 0-5 combined (GEN, JDG, BLG, HLE, JDG at EWC), with only the 2-3 vs HLE close; vs other regions 4-1 in series (LOUD, FURIA, TSW, G2 wins; MIBR.LOS loss). [14][17][22]
