## Identity
How they win: aggressive early game (76.5% first blood) and big mid-game objective fights (Baron fights decided G1 of both Gen.G series and G5 of the MSI final via Kanavi's steal). Zeka (25.1% DMG, 5.0 KDA, 5 solo kills in the page sample) and Gumayusi (27.5% DMG) carry; Delight sits at 76% KP. [8][3][11][16] The 2026 MSI run beat G2 3-0, LYON 3-2, and BLG 3-2 after a 1-3 UB final loss. [15]
How they lose: converting early leads (only 62.5% WR when ahead @15) and getting out-scaled/out-fought by Gen.G (45-minute G2, 30 and 26 minute G3/G4 in the UB final; 28/30/26 in the final). Analysts flag mid-game pacing when early dives fail. [8][11][3][41] Top lane was the weakest stat line (Zeus -170 GD@15, -227 XPD@15 in the 9-game sample). [8]
