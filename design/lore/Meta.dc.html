Draft

Fearless Draft in every Bo3 and Bo5: a champion picked earlier in the series is gone for both teams. Swiss Bo1s use standard draft. Right of First Selection decides side or pick order.

Why so many 3-0 calls

Of 178 Bo5s in 2026, 44% were sweeps, and 3-0 was the most common score at every favourite strength. A 3-0 call is the single likeliest score, not a promise.
